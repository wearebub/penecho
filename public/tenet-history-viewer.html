Open a student's original Tenet PDF or .tenet work file to follow saved page checkpoints, questions, and recorded AI help. You can also open an older Tenet history archive or play the clearly labeled fictional example. No iPad is needed.

Tenet PDF exports embed the complete .tenet work file by default. Students can also share that file separately from Pages & files > Share work. Download the original attachment from your school-approved learning platform, then choose Open shared work here. Ordinary PDF readers show the report; this viewer opens its attached history. Printing or converting a PDF can remove that attachment. LMS preservation must be checked with your district's platform.

This is a sample and local-file preview, not a connected assignment classroom or authenticated teacher dashboard. Your chosen file stays in this browser session. The viewer does not record your canvas, upload work, call AI, or verify independent authorship.

Playback shows coalesced page checkpoints, not every pen stroke. No assignment rules, teacher permissions or Schoology submission are implied.
