Open a student's .tenet work file to follow saved page checkpoints, questions, and recorded AI help. You can also open an older Tenet history archive or play the clearly labeled fictional example. No iPad is needed.

Students create the file from Pages & files > Share work. Download the attachment from your school-approved learning platform, then choose Open shared work here. An optional PDF report is readable without this viewer.

This is a sample and local-file preview, not a connected assignment classroom or authenticated teacher dashboard. Your chosen file stays in this browser session. The viewer does not record your canvas, upload work, call AI, or verify independent authorship.

Playback shows coalesced page checkpoints, not every pen stroke. No assignment rules, teacher permissions or Schoology submission are implied.
